About

A small studio with a specific opinion: your website should just work.

Surprise Studio is a small, independent web development studio. We build websites and web applications for small businesses, for gifts, for anniversary and professionals.

Surprise Studio is all about building a digital place for businesses, gifts, tasks and memories.

We believe that having a digital presence is as good as being known by the people that you love, respect and adore.

Why “Surprise Studio”?

Starting from the name, our first goal is to create a surprise of joy on people. Gifts are the main things that makes people surprised on their special days, therefore, we started to make gifts as a start. But additionally, we wanted to make life easier by letting people have a digital presence of their own on the web and tech world.

How we think

Four values we actually enforce.

Values are cheap until they cost you something. These four occasionally do — and we keep them anyway.

Clarity

Making things clear, easy and simple to understand and use.

Accessibility

Making things accessible and reachable by people on different location, on different status and on different life journey.

Honesty

Making things fair all over the tech world by offering services that recieve what worths them, neither more nor less.

Readiness

Buildint things that are ready to be owned, tranferred and ready enough to start doing the things that they are built for.
